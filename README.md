# Deify card drawer

A free, offline card drawer for *Deify: A Mythical Solo Role-Playing Game* by Allyson D'Antonio (Dover). Fan-made; each card's prompt is in the book.

**Use it:** https://mildlysentient.github.io/deify/ — on iPhone, open it in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen. It runs offline after the first visit.

- Separate Major and Minor Arcana decks, drawn without repeats
- The Fool first, then one Major and three Minor cards per round until The World
- Birth, Prime and Decline phases tracked for Minor prompts
- Fate rule: upright or reversed on every card
- Options: hold The Tower until The Chariot, hold The World, quick game
- Pick 1 of 2 (The High Priestess), extra cards, undo, copyable log
- Save codes to back up a game or move it between devices
